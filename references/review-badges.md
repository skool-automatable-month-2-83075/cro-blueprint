# Review badges

Every page needs two or three review or award badges near the form and in the first screen: the platform's mark, the stars drawn in, the score and the count. They borrow trust from a site the visitor already knows. A copied image of a badge proves less than the platform's own live badge or widget, so point owners to their business account on each platform.

## Which ones fit

- **Local service and trades:** Google, Yelp, Facebook, TrustIndex, Bark, HomeStars.
- **Software and B2B services:** G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Product Hunt.
- **Ecommerce:** Trustpilot, Google, Reviews.io, Judge.me.
